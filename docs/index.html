      How to play

      Find as many different English words as you can in one minute.

      • Trace three or more letters, then lift to submit. You can also tap tiles and press Submit.
      • Letters can touch at the sides or corners. Use a tile once per word. A Qu tile counts as two letters.
      • Each word grows a flower. Share the board link to compare your best scores with friends.
